Your attention is being farmed.
Food companies, feeds, ads, infinite samey content —
all engineered to keep you scrolling, snacking, watching.

This is not a campaign. There is nothing to join.
It is a reminder: real art, real food, real people,
real outdoors, real craft.

You already know. Go outside.

WHY

Attention is the raw material of a life.
What you attend to becomes what you are.

There is an industry whose entire business model
is holding your gaze a few seconds longer.
It is not evil in a cartoon way — it simply works:
the slot machine pays out just often enough.

Nobody is coming to give you your attention back.
Taking it back is a private, unglamorous act:
cooking a real meal, walking without headphones,
making something badly and slowly.

Who owns your nervous system?
